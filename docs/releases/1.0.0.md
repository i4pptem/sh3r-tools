## One workspace. From individual assets to entire scenes.

**Silent Hill 3 Tools reaches 1.0.0.** This release brings whole-scene playback and Blender exchange, an integrated map/collision/camera workspace, assisted pose-morph transfer and compact mods that preserve the original game files on disk.

The changelog below covers **everything new or expanded since 0.9.0**, including the work developed through the intermediate previews. Existing archive, model, texture, audio and movie workflows remain available.

| New in 1.0 | What it brings to your workflow |
| --- | --- |
| **Cutscene Inspector** | See the whole scene, then take its existing animation, camera and light tracks into Blender and back. |
| **World Inspector** | Edit the map, collision and gameplay cameras together in one room workspace. |
| **Blender Morph Tools** | Transfer native expressions to a replacement mesh with landmarks, protected regions and individual previews. |
| **Compact DLL/ASI mods** | Distribute changed archive entries and apply required patches in memory. |
| **Review before applying** | Compare model replacements and inspect the complete mod before building. |

## 🎬 Cutscene Inspector — the whole scene, in time

0.9.0 introduced character-track exchange. **1.0.0 adds a dedicated workspace for complete real-time cutscenes.**

![Cutscene Inspector: synchronized characters, environment and camera in scene 38](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/i4pptem/sh3r-tools/v1.0.0/.github/images/cutscene.png)

- Play characters, skeletal animation, facial morphs, cameras, environment, moving map objects and sound on one timeline.
- Switch between the game camera and a free camera; scrub, loop and inspect individual frames.
- Automatically resolve **72 of 77 scenes** in the tested installation. Resource selectors let you choose character variants, maps and audio when needed.
- **Export a complete scene to `.blend`**, with packed textures/audio and existing character, morph, camera, light and prop animation.
- **Reimport edited scene channels** with a change review before staging. Choose which components to apply; preserve unedited tracks and native scene length.
- Apply PACK visibility masks to matching map groups and hide known gameplay-only character duplicates, including extra weapons on Memory of Alessa.
- Correct opaque wall facing and incorporate supported stage rules, including scene 41 visibility, scene 80 carousel motion and scene 48's audio lead-in.
- Preserve partially transparent secondary materials, including Vincent's glasses, during scene exchange.
- Show export stages, completed frames where available and elapsed time **inside the export dialog**.

Native lighting and scripted effects are approximated. Creating new actors/channels or changing scene duration is outside this release.

[Cutscene playback and Blender exchange →](https://github.com/i4pptem/sh3r-tools/blob/v1.0.0/docs/CUTSCENES.md)

## 🗺️ World Inspector — edit the room as a whole

Maps now have their own browser, grouped by **20 verified area labels**, with name/code search and direct access to the existing geometry editor.

![World Inspector: map geometry with collision and camera reference layers](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/i4pptem/sh3r-tools/v1.0.0/.github/images/world.png)

- Overlay matching **MAP, CLD and CAM** resources and edit them in the same viewport.
- Move layer controls into **Inspector → Layers**; keep collision, camera and rebuild settings in a draggable, resizable **Room editor**.
- Pick visible collision directly. Select connected surfaces, whole native groups or several records; move, rotate and scale them together within native shape constraints.
- Edit gameplay camera zones, anchored camera positions and supported fixed-camera sight angles.
- Study framing through a selected camera with a movable character reference and its own gizmo.
- Bind collision groups to selected map meshes. Regenerate bound collision after transforms or topology changes, with **simplification, walkable-face filtering and a complexity estimate**.
- Apply room edits atomically and undo map/collision/camera operations through a shared history. Build review links back to unapplied rooms and their validation errors.
- Fill the viewport with the map preview and add flight navigation: **WASD**, **X/C** for height, **Shift** for speed and right-drag to look around. **E/R/T** select Move / Rotate / Scale.
- Support expanded static map parts and **full-size embedded/shared map textures**, with complete-stage memory checks and conditional background-storage/transparent-queue patches.

Existing whole-map and individual-part GLB exchange remain available. Room streaming, gameplay events and level scripts are not edited.

[Map, collision and camera workflows →](https://github.com/i4pptem/sh3r-tools/blob/v1.0.0/docs/MAP-WORLD.md)

## 🧩 Blender Morph Tools 1.3.2 — expressions for replacement meshes

The optional add-on is now **included with the application**. Use the highlighted **Get Blender morph add-on…** card on a model with native morphs to save its installer.

![Model workspace with the bundled Blender Morph Tools card and KG1 controls](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/i4pptem/sh3r-tools/v1.0.0/.github/images/workspace.png)

- Choose the original and replacement in a dedicated **1. Meshes** section, with Use active and Select / frame controls.
- Match similar surfaces directly, or use **paired neutral landmarks** with face, hair and cloth templates.
- Pick and label corresponding points in the viewport; detect captured point indices invalidated by topology changes.
- Protect opposing lip rims, eyelids and overlapping layers through separate source/target regions. Generate editable facial-region suggestions and soften their outer boundaries.
- Adjust smoothing and preserve captured landmark motion through local surface corrections.
- **Preview one morph at a time** on side-by-side temporary copies, with previous/next navigation, a shared amount slider and region coverage.
- Highlight/select unmatched vertices; keep them unchanged instead of deforming them from unrelated surfaces.
- Adjust inward/outward facial depth **per expression and region** when different lip proportions need correction.
- Create all keys on a new copy while preserving Basis, topology, UVs and skin weights; input objects stay unchanged.

The supplied Heather face workflow has user confirmation in **Blender 5.2.2 on macOS**. Other faces, hair and cloth still need their own visual review. The add-on transfers expressions; it does not create missing anatomy, a new rig or new game morph slots.

[Install and use Blender Morph Tools →](https://github.com/i4pptem/sh3r-tools/blob/v1.0.0/docs/BLENDER-MORPH-TOOLS.md)

## 📦 Compact mods — keep the original files on disk

**Build mod → ASI overlay** adds a second delivery mode alongside replacement files. Required runtime extensions run in memory, and changed assets live in a separate mod folder.

```text
Silent Hill 3/
├── sh3.exe                      original executable
├── data/                        original game archives
└── plugins/
    ├── SH3ToolsLoader.asi        small initialization bridge
    ├── SH3Tools.dll              runtime patches and asset loading
    └── SH3Tools/
        ├── SH3Tools.patch
        ├── SH3Tools.assets
        └── data/                changed assets only
```

- Package **only changed ARC/AFS entries**, rather than rebuilding and distributing each entire source archive.
- Support loose replacements under **movie, pic and sound** as well as archived assets.
- Apply only the runtime extensions required by the staged output; the original executable on disk is preserved.
- Validate source and replacement identities before activation.
- Use **Merge mods** to combine different assets from saved projects, supported builds, archives and portable `.sh3mod` packages, with explicit conflict choices.
- Regenerate loader files and required patches from the merged changes when building.

A compatible **Win32 Ultimate ASI Loader** and matching original archives/executable are required. Follow the generated installation instructions for the loader and `global.ini`. Changed asset files are still included in the mod; this is not binary-delta distribution. Two edits inside the same MDL/MAP still require choosing one file.

[Overlay setup and compatibility →](https://github.com/i4pptem/sh3r-tools/blob/v1.0.0/docs/ASI-OVERLAY.md)

## Review, shadows and animation

### See the replacement before applying it

- Compare the **current model and exact serialized replacement** in synchronized views before staging.
- Inspect geometry, textures, UVs, wireframe, individual parts, morphs and original ANM/PACK motion.
- Highlight invalid weights and suspicious bone/visibility associations. Missing textures and invalid weights block staging.
- Open a shared **Build review** with changed assets/sizes, errors, warnings, pending room edits and the reasons each runtime patch is required.
- Retain unsupported-animation-channel reports in the project and surface them during build review.

### Author character shadow proxies

- Resolve the KG1 actually used by the native actor/costume tables, including Heather's shared `pl_htr_alh.kg1`.
- Rebuild simplified character shadow volumes from replacement geometry, covering existing shadow blocks.
- Inspect the proxy over the model; **export/import individual bone objects through GLB**.
- Explicitly disable a shadow object or restore its opened-source geometry. Report shared-resource conflicts before staging.
- Validate changed geometry after native coordinate quantization and preserve untouched original objects.

KG1 remains rigid per bone. Facial morph deformation and blended shadow skinning are not implemented; face/joint self-shadowing can need manual proxy adjustment.

### Make animation support easier to understand

- Add **Channel support…** with the actual writable Position/Rotation components for every original bone in the selected ANM.
- Add readable names for **43 verified Heather Action states**; retain numeric IDs for unverified entries.
- Add **Auto-play** so one-shot actions can repeat during model preview.
- Retain the IK/FK, helper-bone filtering, cross-bank transfer and Blender/FBX workflows introduced in 0.9.0.

ANM length and channel layout remain native. **Fit** resamples an edited clip into an existing range; missing native channels cannot be created by adding Blender keys.

[Model/build review, channel tables and KG1 editing →](https://github.com/i4pptem/sh3r-tools/blob/v1.0.0/docs/AUTHORING-REVIEW.md)

## Download and get started

| File | Choose this for |
| --- | --- |
| **Silent-Hill-3-Tools-1.0.0-win-x64.zip** | The runnable Windows application, including Blender Morph Tools 1.3.2. |
| **Silent-Hill-3-Tools-1.0.0-source.zip** | The public source code and build instructions. |
| **SHA256SUMS.txt** | Checking the downloaded archives. |

1. Extract the **entire Windows ZIP** and run **Silent Hill 3 Tools.exe**. No Node.js installation is needed.
2. For movie/media conversion, close the app and run **Install media.cmd** once to download the verified optional FFmpeg runtime.
3. Install **Blender 4.2+** for FBX and `.blend` workflows; Blender 5.2.2 was used for validation. Install the bundled morph add-on separately through Blender's Add-ons preferences if you want to transfer expressions.
4. Choose **Open data folder**, select the game's `data` directory, make your edits and save a `.sh3project`.
5. Review and stage your replacements, then **Build mod**. Choose a delivery mode and follow its installation instructions with the game closed.

The main application is **Windows x64**. The Blender add-on also works independently on macOS. RTX Remix is not required. No game executable or game archives are included in the tool release.

<details>
<summary><b>Updating from 0.9.0 or an intermediate preview</b></summary>

Extract 1.0.0 into a new folder, keep projects/exports outside the application directory and retain your previous version until you have checked your active work. Install or copy the verified optional media component as described in the installation guide.

**Only for users of the experimental Action-length extension:** that feature was removed during development. Restore the original affected ANM and executable, then reimport into an existing native range. Normal fixed-range animation import remains available.

Older ASI overlay layouts should be migrated using the generated instructions. Keep one active SH3Tools runtime; use Merge mods to combine supported changes.

[Installation and rollback](https://github.com/i4pptem/sh3r-tools/blob/v1.0.0/docs/INSTALL.md)

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Validation and current boundaries</b></summary>

The release passed **348 automated application tests**, **four Blender morph test suites**, repository checks, source-ZIP installation/build and packaged/extracted application smoke checks. The actual Inspector add-on export was checked against the bundled 1.3.2 source.

These checks do not prove every authored mod works in every scene. Model replacement retains the original skeleton. Cutscene duration/new actors/channels, native ANM channel creation, full level scripting and internal MDL/MAP conflict merging are outside 1.0. KG1 does not follow facial morphs. Test relevant gameplay, weapon states, room transitions and cutscenes before distributing your mod.
